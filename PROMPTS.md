
# Prompts

## AI Summary Prompt

Generate a concise 100-word summary explaining:
- current AI spending
- estimated savings
- recommended optimizations
- potential annual impact

Tone:
Professional, analytical, startup-focused.

---

## Why This Prompt

The prompt focuses on clarity and actionable recommendations instead of generic AI-generated marketing language.

---

## Failed Attempts

- Long summaries reduced readability.
- Aggressive cost-cutting recommendations felt unrealistic.
